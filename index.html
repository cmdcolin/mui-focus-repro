MUI Menu in an iframe scrolls the page around it

This browser is not Firefox, so the page will not jump here. The panel still shows MUI focusing the row before the menu has a position.

@mui/material 9.5.0 focuses a Menu's first row before Popover moves the menu beside its button. Firefox scrolls the page around an iframe to show a focused element, so this page jumps.

To reproduce, in Firefox

  1. Click Run test at the bottom right. It scrolls the yellow bar at the top of the dashed iframe off the top of the window, then opens the menu.
  2. The page snaps back up until the yellow bar is on screen, and the panel says how far the page jumped. The menu you opened is now below the window.
  3. Switch to With the workaround and run it again: the page stays put and the menu opens beside its button.

By hand: scroll down until the yellow bar has just gone off the top of the window and the blue Open menu button is visible, then click it.
